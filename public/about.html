About Us

Independent news, reported clearly

SHEREBLOG NEWS is a digital news publication covering politics, business, entertainment, sports, technology, and world news — reported fast, and reported straight.

SHEREBLOG NEWS was founded to give readers a source of news they can trust: stories checked before they're published, headlines that describe what actually happened, and a newsroom that corrects its mistakes openly when it gets something wrong.

We publish across a wide range of beats — Politics, Entertainment, Sports, Business, Technology, World News, Lifestyle, Health, Education, Crime, and Opinion — with a team of editors and contributing authors working to keep our coverage current, accurate, and useful to the communities we serve.

What we stand for

Accuracy first

Every story is reviewed before it goes live. When we get something wrong, we correct it and say so.

Independent voice

Our reporting isn't for sale. Editorial decisions are made by our editors, not by advertisers or outside interests.

Community-driven

Readers can submit tips and stories directly to our newsroom — every submission is reviewed by a real editor.

Our newsroom

SHEREBLOG NEWS operates with a small, focused editorial team of editors, authors, and moderators, supported by an admin dashboard built specifically for fast, accurate daily publishing. We take article accuracy, source attribution, and reader privacy seriously — see our Editorial Policy and Privacy Policy for details.

Got a story?

If you have a tip, a story, or information the public should know, we want to hear from you. Visit our Submit News page, or reach out directly via our Contact Us page.
