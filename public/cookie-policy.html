Cookie Policy

How SHEREBLOG NEWS uses cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how SHEREBLOG NEWS uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They're widely used to make websites function properly, to remember your preferences, and to understand how a site is used.

2. How We Use Cookies

SHEREBLOG NEWS keeps cookie use to a minimum. Specifically:

  • Essential/session cookies: used in our admin dashboard to keep editors and administrators securely signed in. These are not set for ordinary readers browsing the public site.
  • No third-party advertising or tracking cookies: we do not run ad networks and we do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of our readers.

3. Local Storage

Our admin dashboard uses your browser's local storage (not a cookie, but a similar browser technology) to keep you signed in between visits. This only applies to staff logging into the admin area at /admin, not to readers of the public site.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Since SHEREBLOG NEWS's public-facing site does not rely on cookies for core reading functionality, disabling cookies should not affect your ability to read our articles.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our site evolves. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

6. Contact

Questions about our use of cookies can be sent to shereblognews@gmail.com.
