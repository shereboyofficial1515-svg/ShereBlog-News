Editorial Policy

How we report, source, review, and correct the news we publish.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Editorial Independence

SHEREBLOG NEWS's editorial decisions — what we cover, how we cover it, and when we publish — are made independently by our editorial team. Advertisers, sponsors, and outside parties have no influence over our reporting or editorial judgment.

2. Sourcing Standards

We aim to attribute information to its original source wherever possible. Where a story is based on a reader submission, an official statement, or another outlet's reporting, we credit it accordingly using the article's Source field.

3. Review Before Publication

Every article passes through our editorial workflow before publication:

  • Draft: an author writes and prepares the story.
  • Pending Review: the story is submitted for an editor's review.
  • Published: only an editor, admin, or super admin can move a story to published — authors cannot self-publish directly to the live site.

4. Reader Submissions

Stories and tips submitted by readers through our Submit News page are never published automatically. Every submission is reviewed by a member of our editorial team, who may approve it for further development into a full article, request more information, or decline it.

5. Corrections Policy

When we get something wrong, we fix it. If a factual error is identified after publication:

  • The article is corrected as soon as possible after verification.
  • Material corrections are reflected in the article's "Updated" timestamp, visible alongside the original publish date.
  • Readers can report suspected errors to shereblognews@gmail.com.

6. Opinion vs. News Reporting

We clearly separate news reporting from opinion content. Articles published under our Opinion category represent the views of the individual author and are labeled accordingly — they do not represent SHEREBLOG NEWS's institutional position.

7. Comment Moderation

Reader comments are reviewed before appearing publicly. We do not permit harassment, hate speech, defamation, or spam, and reserve the right to remove any comment or disable commenting entirely, per our Terms of Service.

8. Contact Our Editorial Team

For editorial inquiries, corrections, or feedback on our coverage, reach us at shereblognews@gmail.com or via our Contact page.
