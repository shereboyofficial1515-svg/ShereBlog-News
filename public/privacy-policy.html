Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when you use SHEREBLOG NEWS.

Last updated: September 2026

SHEREBLOG NEWS ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few limited ways:

  • Newsletter subscriptions: your email address, when you choose to subscribe.
  • News submissions: your name, email, phone number (optional), and the story details you choose to submit through our Submit News form.
  • Comments: your name and email address, when you leave a comment on an article. Your email is never displayed publicly.
  • Basic usage data: article view counts, used only in aggregate to understand which stories readers find valuable. We do not build individual reader profiles from this data.

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through this site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send newsletter emails to subscribers who have confirmed their subscription (double opt-in).
  • To review and, where appropriate, follow up on news submissions.
  • To moderate comments before they appear publicly.
  • To understand aggregate readership trends and improve our coverage.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details on how we use cookies and similar technologies.

4. Data Retention

We retain newsletter subscriber data until you unsubscribe. News submissions and comments are retained as part of our editorial and moderation records. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us (see below).

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To unsubscribe from our newsletter, use the unsubscribe link in any email or visit our unsubscribe page.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party websites, including social media platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted password storage, secure authentication, and access controls — to protect the data we hold. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to shereblognews@gmail.com or via our Contact page.
